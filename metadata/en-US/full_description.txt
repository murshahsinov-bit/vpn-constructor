<p>ShieldVPN is a free VPN client for Android built on the open source Xray core. It hides behind a single big "Connect" button: the app measures the latency of every server from your subscription, picks the fastest reachable one and only then brings the tunnel up. No account setup on a website, no ads, no analytics.</p><p><b>What you get</b></p><ul><li><b>Smart auto-selection</b> - all servers are probed on launch and the fastest one is dialed automatically. Open the server list to see the country flag, the node name and the latency in milliseconds, colour coded from green to red.</li><li><b>Local DPI bypass for YouTube</b> - a bundled ByeDPI (ciadpi) process listens on 127.0.0.1:1080 and only the YouTube domains (youtube.com, youtu.be, googlevideo.com, ytimg.com and friends) are routed through it. The rest of your traffic keeps using the encrypted VLESS/Reality tunnel, and the system VPN is never hijacked by the bypass.</li><li><b>Subscriptions that stay fresh</b> - the app ships with a subscription that is regenerated continuously from public VLESS/Reality sources and can be refreshed from the connect screen.</li><li><b>Sign-in gate</b> - access is only granted after signing in with Google or with an e-mail address, so the subscription token can follow the account to another device. In builds without Firebase credentials the same screen works offline and keeps the password hash on the device only.</li><li><b>Protocol support</b> - VLESS with Reality, XHTTP, TCP+TLS/Vision, VMess, Trojan, Shadowsocks, SOCKS, WireGuard and Hysteria2, imported from standard share links or a subscription.</li></ul><p><b>How it works</b></p><p>ShieldVPN runs Xray as a local VPN service. Profiles come from share links, QR codes or subscriptions, and routing rules can be edited from the advanced screen. The Xray core, the geo databases and the ByeDPI binary are all free software and are fetched by <i>scripts/fetch-libs.sh</i> when the project is built, so the repository contains no prebuilt binaries.</p><p><b>Privacy</b></p><p>The app contains no advertising or tracking SDKs. The only network endpoints it contacts are the subscription URL it was built with, the servers from that subscription, and - if the build was compiled with Firebase credentials - Google's Firebase Auth/Firestore to keep your account and subscription token in sync. Builds without those credentials never talk to Google's backend at all.</p><p><b>Free software</b></p><p>ShieldVPN is a fork of v2rayNG and is distributed under the GNU General Public License v3.0. Because a VPN client is security software, you are welcome to read, audit and rebuild it.</p>
